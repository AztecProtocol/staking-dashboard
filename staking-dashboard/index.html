The Aztec Staking Dashboard

This is the official dashboard for staking $AZTEC, the token of Aztec Network. Staked tokens back the sequencers that run the network, and stakers earn rewards. The dashboard shows the total value staked, the estimated APR, the number of active sequencers and the minimum stake, all read live from the network.

Two ways to stake

  • Delegate to a provider. Choose a staking provider, delegate your tokens to it and earn rewards without running any infrastructure. The provider list shows each provider's total stake and commission.
  • Run your own sequencer. Register your sequencer's keys and stake to your own node. This gives you full control and needs a running sequencer and the experience to operate it.

Each stake position needs the network's minimum stake, currently 200,000 $AZTEC. To stake a smaller amount, use liquid staking with Olla, which has no minimum.

After connecting a wallet you can see your positions, follow your rewards and claim them. You can stake from a wallet balance or from a Token Vault, using a browser wallet or a Safe multisig. Staking happens through smart contracts on Ethereum.
